# Suggested Study Plan

3-4 weeks, ~45 min/day. Per topic: `theory/` → `snippets/` → `output-based/` → `scenarios/` → `problems/` → `interview-qa/`.

## Week 1 — Type fundamentals
- Day 1: `01-basic-types`
- Day 2: `02-interfaces-type-aliases`
- Day 3: `03-functions-overloads`
- Day 4–5: `04-union-intersection-types` (discriminated unions come up constantly — don't rush)
- Day 6–7: `05-generics` (the topic most people are weakest on — spend real time here)

## Week 2 — OOP & literal types
- Day 1–2: `06-classes-access-modifiers`
- Day 3: `07-enums-literal-types`
- Day 4–5: `08-type-narrowing-guards`
- Day 6–7: Review — `output-based/` for weeks 1-2

## Week 3 — Advanced type system
- Day 1–2: `09-utility-types`
- Day 3–5: `10-advanced-types-mapped-conditional` (infer, conditional types — the hardest topic here, budget extra time)
- Day 6: `11-modules-namespaces`
- Day 7: `12-type-inference-assertions` (the `satisfies` operator is a newer favorite interview question)

## Week 4 — Config & applied TS
- Day 1–2: `13-tsconfig-strict-mode`
- Day 3–4: `14-typescript-with-react`
- Day 5–7: Full review — every `problems/` folder, redo the ones you didn't get right the first time

## Interview-week fast pass

1. `output-based/` for `05-generics`, `08-type-narrowing-guards`, `10-advanced-types-mapped-conditional` — the type-system questions that separate "used TS" from "understands TS"
2. `problems/` across the whole repo — "implement X from scratch" is asked constantly for TS roles
3. `scenarios/` for `02-interfaces-type-aliases`, `04-union-intersection-types`, `09-utility-types` — realistic "how would you model this" questions
