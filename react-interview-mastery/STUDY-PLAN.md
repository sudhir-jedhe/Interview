# Suggested Study Plan

A 4-week plan, ~45–60 min/day. Do this after (or alongside) the core JS repo.

## Week 1 — Core mechanics
- Day 1: `01-jsx-rendering-basics`
- Day 2: `02-components-props`
- Day 3–4: `03-state-usestate`
- Day 5–6: `04-useeffect-lifecycle` (spend extra time here — it's the most-tested topic)
- Day 7: `05-event-handling-forms`

## Week 2 — Rendering & hooks
- Day 1: `06-lists-keys-conditional-rendering`
- Day 2: `07-context-api`
- Day 3: `08-useref-dom-access`
- Day 4: `09-usememo-usecallback`
- Day 5–6: `10-custom-hooks`
- Day 7: Review — output-based questions for topics 1–10

## Week 3 — Performance, patterns & routing
- Day 1–2: `11-rerenders-performance`
- Day 3: `12-hoc-render-props`
- Day 4: `13-error-boundaries`
- Day 5–6: `14-react-router`
- Day 7: Review — scenario questions for topics 6–14

## Week 4 — Modern React & polish
- Day 1–2: `15-data-fetching-patterns`
- Day 3: `16-suspense-code-splitting`
- Day 4–5: `17-react-18-19-features` (Server Components questions come up a lot now — don't skip)
- Day 6: `18-design-patterns-anti-patterns`
- Day 7: Full review — every `06-interview-questions.md`

## Interview-week fast pass

1. `03-output-based-questions.md` for `04-useeffect-lifecycle`, `03-state-usestate`, `09-usememo-usecallback`, `11-rerenders-performance` (this is where most React interviews actually probe)
2. `05-scenario-questions.md` for `06-lists-keys-conditional-rendering`, `07-context-api`, `15-data-fetching-patterns`
3. `04-comparisons.md` across the whole repo
4. Skim `../js_polyfills/React/Machine Codding` and `React 19` folders (see SOURCE-MAP.md) — you already have build-it-yourself practice problems there
