# Suggested Study Plan

A 5-week plan assuming ~45–60 minutes a day. Adjust pace freely — the point is to move topic by topic instead of jumping around.

## Week 1 — Foundations
- Day 1–2: `01-js-basics-data-types`, `02-scope-hoisting`
- Day 3–4: `03-functions-this`, `05-call-apply-bind`
- Day 5: `04-closures`
- Day 6–7: Review — redo all `output-based/` files for topics 1–5 without looking at answers first

## Week 2 — Data structures
- Day 1–2: `06-objects-prototypes`
- Day 3: `07-classes-oop`
- Day 4–5: `08-arrays`
- Day 6: `09-strings-numbers-math`
- Day 7: Review `theory/` comparisons + `interview-qa/` for topics 6–9

## Week 3 — Syntax & modern JS
- Day 1: `10-operators-coercion`
- Day 2: `11-destructuring-spread-rest`
- Day 3: `12-loops-iterators`
- Day 4–5: `13-es6-plus`
- Day 6–7: Review — `output-based/` for topics 10–13 (coercion questions especially, they're the most commonly missed)

## Week 4 — Async & the runtime
- Day 1–3: `14-async-js` (this is the biggest topic — give it extra time)
- Day 4–5: `15-event-loop`
- Day 6: `16-error-handling`
- Day 7: Review — `scenarios/` for topics 14–16

## Week 5 — Browser, patterns & polish
- Day 1–2: `17-dom-events-browser-apis`
- Day 3: `18-design-patterns-polyfills`
- Day 4: `19-memory-performance`
- Day 5: `20-security-basics`
- Day 6–7: Full-repo review — every `interview-qa/` folder, timed self-quiz

## Interview-week fast pass (2–3 days before)

Skip `theory/` entirely. Only do, in this order:
1. `output-based/` for every topic (fastest signal on whether you actually understand execution order, closures, `this`, and coercion)
2. `scenarios/` for 14, 15, 08, 06 (async, event loop, arrays, objects — these come up most)
3. The comparison tables inside `theory/` across the repo (quick to skim, high recall value)
4. `problems/` if you have time — "implement X from scratch" comes up constantly
