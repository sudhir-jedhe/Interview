This infographic, titled **"99 Claude (Secret) Commands"** by Ajit Kumar, is structured into **9 distinct categories**, offering prompt templates and slash commands to optimize various workflows using Claude.

Here is an elaboration of what each category covers:

---

### 1. Email & Messages

* **Focus:** Streamlining professional correspondence, rejections, updates, and follow-ups.
* **Key Commands:**
* `/DECLINE [email]` – Writes a polite refusal.
* `/SHORTEN [email]` – Cuts length by half while keeping core points.
* `/WARMER [email]` – Makes text less stiff.
* `/FOLLOWUP [email]` – Drafts a nudge for an unanswered email.
* Other commands handle setting out-of-office auto-replies (`/TODO`), hard/respectful messages (`/HARASS`), and double-opt-in introductions (`/INTRO`).

### 2. Learn & Understand

* **Focus:** Simplifying complex topics, accelerating learning curves, and organizing mental models.
* **Key Commands:**
* `/ELI10 [topic]` – Explains a concept as if to a ten-year-old.
* `/PRIMER [topic]` – Provides a quick crash course.
* `/MYTHS [topic]` – Debunks common misconceptions.
* `/ANALOGY [concept]` – Generates a real-world comparison.
* Other prompts include creating 5-question quizzes (`/QUIZ`), contrasting differences (`/COMPARE`), and unlocking mental models (`/MENTALMODEL`).

### 3. Work & Career

* **Focus:** Professional development, job hunting, interviewing, and compensation negotiations.
* **Key Commands:**
* `/INTERVIEWQ [questions]` – Generates confident answer preparations.
* `/RESUMEBULLET [bullet]` – Rewrites and improves resume bullet points.
* `/ASKINTERVIEWER [role + company type]` – Suggests smart questions to ask at the end of an interview.
* `/NEGOTIATE [the ask]` – Scripts salary or contract negotiations.
* Other commands help explain career gaps (`/GAP`), draft LinkedIn reach-outs (`/RECONNECT`), and script raise requests (`/RAISE`).

### 4. Write & Edit

* **Focus:** Refining text quality, adjusting tone, fixing grammar, and experimenting with structure.
* **Key Commands:**
* `/PROOF [text]` – Fixes typos and grammatical errors.
* `/REWRITES [sentence]` – Generates five alternative versions.
* `/KILLER [text]` – Flags weak words.
* `/HUMAN [text]` – Makes AI-sounding text read naturally.
* Other commands simplify text for a 12-year-old reading level (`/SIMPLIFY`), adjust tone (`/TONE`), and provide title options (`/TITLES`).

### 5. Plan & Organize

* **Focus:** Time management, scheduling, logistics, and productivity tracking.
* **Key Commands:**
* `/WEEK [priorities]` – Helps plan out weekly priorities.
* `/MILESTONES [goal + deadline]` – Sets weekly checkpoints toward a goal.
* `/PACK [trip]` – Generates a comprehensive packing list.
* `/ROUTINE [goal]` – Builds a daily routine.
* Other commands sequence tasks (`/ORDER`), create dinner menus (`/MEALS`), and organize meeting agendas (`/AGENDA`).

### 6. Content & Social

* **Focus:** Copywriting, social media engagement, and marketing hooks.
* **Key Commands:**
* `/HOOK [topic]` – Generates five scroll-stopping openers.
* `/CAPTION [photo/topic]` – Writes short, punchy social media captions.
* `/THREAD [idea]` – Structures a six-tweet thread.
* `/CARO [topic]` – Outlines an image carousel layout.
* Other commands draft calls-to-action (`/CTA`), professional bios (`/BIO`), and sharp comment replies (`/COMMENT`).

### 7. Think & Decide

* **Focus:** Critical thinking, weighing options, decision-making, and risk management.
* **Key Commands:**
* `/CHOOSE [A vs B]` – Weighs options objectively.
* `/REALDEC [decision]` – Outlines pros and cons.
* `/STEELMAN [view]` – Builds the strongest version of an opposing argument.
* `/DEVIL [idea]` – Acts as a devil's advocate.
* Other frameworks include mapping second-order consequences (`/RIPPLE`), pre-mortem analysis (`/PREMORTEM`), and breaking problems down step-by-step (`/5STEPS`).

### 8. Brainstorm

* **Focus:** Creative ideation, problem-solving from different perspectives, and out-of-the-box thinking.
* **Key Commands:**
* `/IDEAS20 [topic]` – Rapidly generates twenty distinct ideas.
* `/GIFTS [person + occasion + budget]` – Brainstorms personalized gift options.
* `/UNUSUAL [problem]` – Suggests ten unconventional approaches.
* `/COMBINE [A + B]` – Blends two unrelated concepts into a new direction.
* Other commands use metaphors (`/METAPHOR`), non-boring conversation starters (`/STARTERS`), and perspective shifts like solving a problem "how a curious kid would" (`/CHILD`).

### 9. Meetings & Notes

* **Focus:** Summarizing discussions, extracting action items, and preparing briefings.
* **Key Commands:**
* `/MEETINGNOTES [transcript]` – Transforms raw notes into clean, structured minutes.
* `/ACTIONSTEMS [notes]` – Pulls out precise to-dos and owners.
* `/BRIEFME [topic + paste]` – Creates a fast pre-meeting briefing.
* `/TLDR [paste]` – Summarizes text into the shortest possible version.
* Other formats include daily update templates (`/STANDBY`), meeting recaps (`/RECAP`), and retrospective templates (`/RETRO`).
