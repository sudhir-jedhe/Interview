Want to boost your website’s speed and user experience? Here are some key terms to understand:

👉 Time To First Byte (TTFB)
🔹 The time it takes for your browser to receive the first byte of data from the server.
🔹 How long you wait before your browser starts getting any information from the website.
 Example: It’s like the time it takes for a waiter to bring your menu to the table after you sit down at a restaurant.

👉 First Paint (FP)
🔹 The time when the first pixel of content appears on the screen.
🔹 The moment you first see something—like a background color or border on the page.
🔹Example: It’s like seeing the first light turn on in a dark room.

👉 First Contentful Paint (FCP)
🔹 The time it takes for the first piece of meaningful content to appear.
🔹 How soon you see the first real content, like text or images, on the page.
🔹Example: It’s like the first time you see the title of a book on the cover after opening it.

👉 Largest Contentful Paint (LCP)
🔹 The time it takes for the largest piece of content (like a big image or block of text) to fully load.
🔹 When the biggest part of the page you’re waiting for is completely visible.
 🔹 Example: It’s like the moment you see the full cover of a magazine after flipping through it.

👉 Time To Interactive (TTI)
🔹 The time when the page becomes fully interactive and responds to user actions.
🔹 When the page is ready to use and you can click buttons or fill out forms without delay.
🔹 Example: It’s like the moment when you can start using a vending machine after it finishes setting up and you can press the buttons to select your snack.
